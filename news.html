12 November 2026Battle Pass

The Darkholm Pass

A monthly battle pass with daily and weekly quests, 30 tiers of rewards and a season leaderboard. Open it with /pass.

  • Three daily quests reset at midnight and three weekly quests reset on Monday (Pacific time): mining, farming, fishing, slaying, building, voting and more.
  • Every 1,000 XP is a new tier. The free track has crate keys, golden apples, XP bottles, Mending and other enchanted books, a totem and six titles.
  • Donor ranks also get a cosmetic premium track: banners, armor trims and four more titles.
  • Wear a title next to your name in chat with /pass titles.
  • Everything resets on the 1st of each month, and the season's top three earn a title for that month.
  • Quests don't count in the Great Hall.

11 November 2026Great Hall

The Great Hall carries your pack

In the Great Hall you now hold the inventory of the realm you just left.

  • Come in from OneBlock and you have your OneBlock gear; come in from Darkholm and you have your Darkholm gear.
  • Anything you gain or use in the Great Hall, such as crate rewards and keys, is saved back to that realm.
  • Realms still keep separate inventories from each other.
