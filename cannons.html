TNT Cannons

⚜

Three water cannons, built and fired on Darkholm itself (Paper 26.3) on the 11th of November 2026. All three share one frame. They differ only in how many charge dispensers you add, so learn one and you can build all three.

How they workBasicLong-rangeAdjustableTest resultsFiring

How they work

  • Charges. Press the button and the charge dispensers drop lit TNT into a water channel. The water carries them to the end. TNT that explodes in water breaks no blocks, so the cannon survives every shot.
  • Projectile. A line of nine repeaters waits about 3.6 seconds, then a dispenser at the front drops one lit TNT onto a slab, just above and ahead of the charges.
  • Launch. A moment later the charges explode and throw the projectile up and forward at about 45°. It explodes where it lands, about 3.6 seconds later.
  • Range comes from the number of charges. Two charges land about 40 blocks out, three about 60. Four or more throw it so high it bursts in the air.

Diagrams: top-down, north at the top, cannon firing east. Numbers count blocks from the water source. To aim another way, rotate every layer together. Hover a square for its block name.

Long-range cannon3 charge dispensers

One more charge dispenser for about 60 blocks, the furthest it reaches while still hitting the ground. Set the last repeater in the line (the one nearest the projectile end) to 2 ticks instead of 4. The projectile then explodes a little sooner, so it detonates on the ground instead of just above it.

Tested range: 54 to 62 blocks with the last repeater at 2 ticks; both shots hit the ground.

Test results on Darkholm

Fired over a flat stone strip. Distance is from the water source to the centre of the crater.

ChargesRepeater lineResult
19 × 4 ticksProjectile only hops. Not enough.
29 × 4 ticksGround hits at 40 and 37.5 blocks
39 × 4 ticksGround hit at 62.5 blocks; two other shots burst a few blocks above the ground near 58–59
38 × 4 + 1 × 2 ticksGround hits at 62 and 54.5 blocks
49 × 4 ticksAirburst about 80 blocks out, 34 blocks up
59 × 4 ticksAirburst about 98 blocks out, 36 blocks up
36 × 4 or 3 × 4 ticksDid not launch: the projectile only hopped

Keep the full nine repeaters at 4 ticks. Shorter delays failed in our tests on this server. Expect a few blocks of spread from shot to shot; TNT never lands in exactly the same place twice.

Firing and safety

  1. Load every charge dispenser you want to use, plus the projectile dispenser, with TNT.
  2. Stand behind the cannon (west of the button), never in front of it.
  3. Press the button once. Wait for the shot to land (about 7 seconds) before pressing again.
  4. Each shot uses one TNT per loaded charge dispenser, plus one for the projectile.

Raiding rules are in the Factions chapter of the Codex.
