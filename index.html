Three Gates, Three Realms

⚜

Every journey starts in the Great Hall. Pick a gate. Each realm keeps its own inventory, armour, XP, health and ender chest. Your coin and Vote Keys come with you everywhere.

West Gate
Darkholm

Factions survival on hard difficulty. Found a faction, claim chunks, set relations and fight for land. Power grows 5 per hour and drops 1 per death.

/f create <name>

North Gate
The Guild

The Adventurers' Guild and every EliteMobs world. Story dungeons, lairs, enchantment challenges and the Guild Exchange. Deaths here cost no faction power.

/ag

East Gate
OneBlock

Your own island, grown one block at a time through each phase. Invite friends to build it with you.

What Awaits Thee

⚜

The Castle

Daily chests around the fountain, four corner towers with lifts, a garden, stables and a smithy. No fall, fire or drowning damage on the castle grounds.

/warp darkholm

The Portal Hall

A portal for every dungeon. Story dungeons, lairs and the Steamworks on Normal, Hard or Mythic.

/warp portalhall

Duels

Queue or challenge a player in Sword, Netherite, Axe, Mace or Crystal across four arenas. Kits are provided, and duels cost no faction power.

/pvp

mcMMO skills

Level skills and climb the leaderboards. Fell whole wild trees with an axe (sneak to cut a single log).

/mcstats

Player auction house

No admin shop. Everything for sale was found or crafted by a player. Explore the wilds, then sell what you bring back.

/ah

The Darkholm Pass

Three daily and three weekly quests, 30 tiers of keys, books and titles, and a monthly leaderboard. Free for everyone; donor ranks also get a cosmetic track.

/pass

The Herald

Ask a question in chat and the Herald answers. It covers factions, travel, combat, ranks, voting and more.

The Road In

How to Join

⚜

Java and Bedrock players share the same world. Every Java version can connect.

Java Edition

1. Open Minecraft and choose Multiplayer, then Add Server.

2. Server address:

play.darkholm.live

3. Save and join. You'll arrive in the Great Hall.

Bedrock Edition

Phone, tablet, Windows or console. Go to Servers, then Add Server.

Address:

bedrock.darkholm.live

Port:

8178

First steps

Read the panels in the Great Hall, then pick a gate. /wild sends you to a random spot in the wilds (5 minute cooldown). Set a home with /sethome.

Link your Discord

Run /discord link in game and DM the code to the Darkholm bot in our Discord.

Vote daily

Every vote pays $91, 5 levels and a Vote Key. See the vote page.
