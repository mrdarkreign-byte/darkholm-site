OneBlock · Build Guide

The Loot Flush Farm

⚜

Designed on Astidonius_'s island. One button washes loot and mobs off the magic block toward your attack window, campfires burn what lands on the floor, and every drop ends up in chests. Vanilla redstone only, and ghast-proof.

How it works

  • Mine from the booth. You stand in a pit one block below the farm and break the magic block through a short tunnel under two iron trapdoors. Mobs can see only your legs, and only the small ones fit through the gap.
  • Press the button to flush. A sticky piston pulls a plug out of the roof for about a second and a half. A water source pours down just north of the magic block and runs south across its top, washing loot and mobs onto the kill spot in front of your window. Then the plug slides back and the water drains. There is no bucket to get out of sync, so you can press as often as you like.
  • Kill at the window. A mob washed onto the kill spot is about 2.7 blocks from your eyes: in sword reach through the tunnel. That spot is the only floor without fire, so the mob stays put.
  • Fire does the rest. Mobs on the soul campfire floor burn. Fire kills drop loot but no XP; land the last hit yourself for XP. Witches drink fire resistance, so flush them to the window and finish them by hand.
  • Everything lands in chests. Hoppers under the campfires and under the kill spot collect through them and feed five double chests in the basement.

Reading the diagrams

  • Each layer is a top-down view with north at the top. Numbers along the edges count blocks from the magic block (★): east is +, south is +.
  • The attack window faces south. If your island needs it facing another way, rotate every layer the same way.
  • Hover a square to see the block name. Layers are numbered from the magic block's own level (0) up and down.

OneBlock rules that shape this build

  • Spawning mobs delete blocks. When the magic block spawns a mob, it clears every block inside that mob's body. Ravagers, big slimes and pandas clear the 3×3 above the magic block. A ghast clears 5×5, four blocks tall. That's why the inside stays empty up to layer +4, and why the valve and wiring live on and above the roof.
  • Water mobs bring water. When a glow squid, axolotl, fish, dolphin, drowned or squid spawns, a water block appears on top of the magic block. Scoop it with a bucket from your pit, or let it be: the next flush washes the mob to your window.
  • Glass near the magic block can vanish. The four optional guide glass blocks and the bottom of the glass pillar sit inside the clearing zone. If a big spawn removes them, put them back.
  • Use stone bricks under redstone, not glass. Redstone needs a solid block under it, and bricks look cleaner on a castle island anyway.

Testing it

  1. With the plug in place and the reservoir full, press the button once from your pit.
  2. Within about a second, water should run across the top of the magic block and onto the kill spot, then stop and drain a few seconds later.
  3. Drop a junk item on top of the magic block and flush. It should wash south, drop through the trapdoor into the hopper and show up in a basement chest.
  4. If the water never stops, the wall torch isn't touching the piston, or the last repeater isn't powering the torch's brick. If it never starts, check that torch 2 lights while the button is pressed.
